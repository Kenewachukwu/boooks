# Concept: The Moon

**Logline:** On a bare golden hill a woman stands under a moon that is crying, and its tears fall as rain over her while the wind pushes everything the same way.

**Why it matters:** It is the picture for Deborah Datoru's poem "The Moon" ("I saw the moon crying", "the infamous reflection of another's purpose") and sets the mood for the whole reading experience: sad, beautiful, and not hopeless.

**Continuity device:** the tear. It wells in the moon's eyes, falls as rain, splashes on the land. (Wind left to right is the second constant.)

**Use:** a seamless 15 s, 9:16 background loop (1080x1920, 30 fps) under the reader's handwritten text. Every cycle divides 15 s, so the last frame flows into the first.

## Loop beats (it repeats, so these are layers, not a story)
| Time | What we see | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| every 2.5 s (eyes offset 1.25 s) | a tear wells in each eye, a drop bulges, detaches and falls through the face | soft plink |
| continuous | rain fans out of the moon's lower rim, slants right, splashes on the land | rain bed |
| continuous | grass and scarf lean right in travelling gusts (5 s and about 2 s cycles) | wind |
| continuous | sky streaks drift right, cloud swirls drift faster, halo breathes (5 s) | low pad |
| 15 s | everything returns to frame 0 | seamless |

No hook or end card: it is a background. Camera is locked.
