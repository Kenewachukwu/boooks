# Style bible: crying moon loop

Read from the four references in `references/` (cobalt and gold oil/gouache paintings, one pastel-lit illustration).

## Grounds
Cobalt sky that brightens toward a peach horizon glow. A bare golden land with a slanted horizon (rises to the right, as in reference 2). Dark blue shadow pools in the foreground (references 3 and 4).

## Texture
Visible bristle brush strokes everywhere: dry-brush streaks across the sky and land, impasto-like cloud swirls, gouache-flat shapes with a few hard edges. Film grain every frame. No gradients without strokes on top.

## Line and shape
Silhouette figure as one flat dark shape with a thin warm rim light on the side facing the moon (references 2 and 3). Grass as tapered blades, long in the foreground, short and sparse on the bare land. Tears as tapered drops with a pale highlight.

## Composition (9:16)
Sky and moon fill the top 45%. The woman stands in the middle band, small against the land but with a clear dark silhouette. Foreground grass frames the bottom and edges.

## Motion rhythm
Slow and heavy. Wind blows left to right: grass leans right in travelling waves, scarf streams right, dust blows right, rain slants right. Tears well and fall every 2.5 s.

## Do-not-copy list
- Reference 1: the clocks, the girl's red coat and the clock-rock world.
- Reference 2: the seated man.
- Reference 3: the flock of birds, the lake and the green meadow.
- Reference 4: the walking man in a blue coat.

Only palette, brush texture, composition style and lone-figure mood are taken. The woman, the crying moon, the tears and the bare land are the user's own subject.
